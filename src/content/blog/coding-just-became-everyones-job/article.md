My daughter Alisa is five. She cannot read yet. In three sessions this August she dictated four
browser games into a microphone, and all four are live in production at
[games.vreshch.com](https://games.vreshch.com) - a
[public repo](https://github.com/vreshch/kids-games), real tests, real deploys.

I went through the transcripts afterwards and counted every human input. There were 67. Eleven were
hers, dictated through a microphone that mangled her relentlessly - "smile" arrived as "file" and
"miles", and the games shipped anyway. Fifty-two were mine, and nearly all of mine were deploys,
tests and analytics. She made the games. I operated them.

![The four games as they look in production: Scary Smile, Perot the parrot, Spranki, and the Crystal Rooms 3D start screen](/blog/coding-just-became-everyones-job/images/games.png)

## What she actually did

The whole skill fits in one loop, and none of it is code:

1. **Say it.** "one smile on the main page. And when you click on it, it became scarier and scarier,
   make it for us."
2. **See it.** She played the build on a phone, minutes after asking.
3. **Correct it.** "Uh, right now, cycle is overlapping with triangle. The placeholder should look
   more like the character itself..."
4. **Accept it.** "really like the game."

She never asked the AI a question - she gave it work and judged the output. And that loop - specify,
then validate - is already the daily work of every profession: a doctor signing off a clinical
summary, a lawyer marking up a draft, an accountant catching the edge case at close.
[Anthropic's study of ~400,000 real coding-agent sessions](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/4zrzovbb/website/b93c7465925dc052b9102209b29b58f11df4fe55.pdf)
found the occupation best at directing coding agents was not software engineering. It was management.

> _Specifying and verifying is the job now. It was always the job. The typing in between just stopped
> being yours._

The full article walks through the receipts: the broken-microphone transcript, when the agent asks
questions and when it silently builds, the typist-vs-secretary precedent for what AI does to
professions, the labor-market numbers, and the two honest boundaries - the platform underneath was
mine, and the validation could never be delegated.
